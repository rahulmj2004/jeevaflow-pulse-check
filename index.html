Measure your pulse with your phone's camera in 30 seconds.

Measure live

  1. Sit down and rest for a minute.
  2. Cover the rear camera and the flash with your index fingertip. Press lightly.
  3. Keep still for 30 seconds.

Or analyse a video

  1. In your camera app, switch the flash to on in video mode.
  2. Cover the lens and flash with your fingertip and record 35 seconds.
  3. Tap Analyse a video and pick that recording.
